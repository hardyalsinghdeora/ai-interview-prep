# Cheatsheets

Quick definitions of the technical and business terms that come up in interviews: 35 sheets with more than 3,000 terms. Use them to look up a term in seconds, or to revise the night before a loop.

These sheets explain terms. To practise answering, use the [question lists by skill](../skills/README.md) and the [topic question banks](../README.md#shared-topics-by-interview-round).

> Items marked *(verify)* can change, such as service names, limits, licences and regulations. Check them against current documentation before you rely on them.
